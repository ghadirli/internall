Internall
=========

1. Drag Internall onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. The FIRST time you open it, do NOT double-click.
   Go to your Applications folder, RIGHT-CLICK Internall, and choose "Open".
   A warning will appear — click "Open" in it.

   After that first time, it opens normally from the Dock or Spotlight.

Why the warning?
   The app is signed, but not notarised by Apple — that needs a paid Apple
   Developer account. macOS shows this warning for any app that isn't.
   Right-clicking and choosing "Open" is how you tell macOS you trust it.
   If you'd rather not, don't install it.

Setting it up
   On first launch it asks for an API key. You need your own, from either
   https://platform.openai.com/api-keys  (OpenAI)
   or
   https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys  (Anthropic)

   Your key is encrypted into the macOS Keychain and is sent only to the
   provider you chose. There is no server in between, and no account to make.
   Voice features need an OpenAI key specifically.

   The app ships with no keys and no chat data in it.
